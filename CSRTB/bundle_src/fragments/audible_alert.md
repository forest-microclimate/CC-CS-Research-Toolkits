## Standing behavior — hands-free audible alert
You fire an audible alert at turn boundaries so the user — who multitasks across several projects at once — hears when your turn ends or needs them. Claude Science has NO turn-end hook (unlike Claude Code's Stop/Notification hooks), so this is AGENT-INITIATED: you fire it yourself as your last action; nothing fires it for you.
- MECHANISM: from a `repl` cell, `host.mcp("hands-free-alert", "beep", sound=<name>, message=<short>)`. This is a custom MCP server on the user's Mac behind a quick tunnel and is OFTEN unreachable (tunnel URLs churn; the server may be off). So the call is FIRE-AND-FORGET: wrap it in `try/except Exception: pass` and swallow everything. A failed, timed-out, or connector-absent beep must NEVER delay the turn, trigger a retry, or be narrated — do not verify reachability first.
- PAUSE-FOR-INPUT: immediately before any `ask_user` — or any turn that ends awaiting the user's answer — fire `sound="twotone"`. Always; a pause always means "come back".
- FULL-STOP: as your genuine last action when SUBSTANTIVE work is complete and nothing is pending, fire `sound="soft"`. Only after real work — NOT on quick one-line replies, acknowledgements, or mid-task clarifications. Beep-spam on trivial turns is the exact annoyance the user rejected; when in doubt, do not beep.
- The connector name `hands-free-alert` is load-bearing (it is the string in the `host.mcp` call); if the user renames the connector, update this line. Sounds are swappable (14 synthesized; soft = stop, twotone = input). Full mechanics + poll-server deployment: load the `audible-alert` skill.
